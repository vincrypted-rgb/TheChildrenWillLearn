> [!WARNING]
> Educators, please be aware that having a scratch account does allow students to interact with projects and other scratch users via commenting, liking, favoriting and remixing projects, as well as following other users. Studios have a designated chat section.
> A user will get a notification to their Scratch inbox each time someone likes, favorites, remixes, or comments on one of their projects. They will also get a notification if they are @mentioned, replied to, followed by a user, or messaged on their profile.

This is your **"coding area" or "workspace."** You can build code with the **blocks** on the left, and it will take place in **The Stage** on the right. 

By clicking on Costumes at the top left, you can open the sprite editor:
<img width="789" height="109" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3bca42a2-c2ed-453f-a7a6-0a6515c3669d" />
<img width="2569" height="1033" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/adbc91b4-e15b-4035-90b0-c764f59a63d0" />
You can draw, rotate, edit, add new or remove sprite costumes in this editor very easily.
It can also convert between vector and bitmap (raster) easily.

Clicking on Sounds opens the sound editor.
<img width="2558" height="1147" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/46b556a1-dab1-4f06-ac24-a6e25215edb8" />
You're able to edit pre-existing/uploaded sounds, or record your own, and then edit them. 

When in the "Code" section, you may notice this button:
<img width="103" height="91" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b23584a8-3f9e-4d89-a691-5901fe27f124" />

The backpack, found towards the bottom of your screen, is a drag and drop copy and paste across projects.
<img width="2693" height="145" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aaeed527-6549-4c8c-b184-3068cac91921" />
It's very helpful if you're going to be reusing the same script, costume, background, etc. It cannot run out of space, but it does get cluttered rather quickly. Right click on an item in your backpack and press delete to remove it.

This section keeps track of all sprites in the project and their current state.
<img width="585" height="559" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f2b3c247-0b92-4d0c-8a0a-75675f93f83a" />
It can be changed manually if you're playing around, but it's most useful to monitor changes made by code that you run.

The share button makes the project visible to other scratch users. See Project Page brings you to this page:
<img width="1210" height="828" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a7d7a39-19be-4336-a982-0d516b09a93f" />

Tutorials and debug are kind of self explanatory. Generally avoid those buttons.
If there's a button that reads "Save Now," that means your project has not saved yet. If it says Project Saved, it saved successfully. I recommend saving often because Scratch does crash sometimes. 
The file button takes you to your "My Stuff" page. It will not save your work before it does.
